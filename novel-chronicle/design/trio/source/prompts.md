## 第一次

工具：`image_edit`，單張參考，16:9。

> Recompose this character sheet into one full-body courtyard lineup, keeping the same warm hand-painted style, the short round boy in the dusty blue wrap tunic, and the taller lean boy in the olive-brown robe. Stand them on sunlit stone in a plain mountain dojo yard: the round boy looks a little winded under a wooden shoulder pole with two half-full buckets, his tunic covering his round torso and a vermilion ribbon in his topknot; the lean boy stands half a head taller, carries fuller buckets with an easy practiced stance, and gives a cool slightly superior sidelong glance. Add a clearly younger smaller girl a step apart, about a head shorter than the round boy, neat black hair tied with a plain cloth, a loose pale grey-green everyday tunic and trousers, clear bright eyes and a shy quiet face, holding one small wooden bucket lightly at her side as if it weighs little. Soft morning light, a water vat and a wooden post behind them, modest coarse cloth, straw sandals, all three full bodies visible.

結果：三人、衣著與負重對照成立。阿祁仍明顯高於「半個頭」，臉偏成熟。

## 第二次（採用）

以第一次結果再編輯，只改身高與阿岫的站姿。

> Keep this same courtyard lineup, clothes, buckets, and the short round winded boy on the left. Redraw the center figure as a lean boy about twelve, only half a head taller than the round boy, still in the olive wrap robe and dark head wrap, with the same cool slightly superior sidelong glance, carrying both buckets easily. Keep the girl on the right a smaller child about eleven in the loose pale tunic, and give her a lighter capable stance: neat hair with a plain cloth tie, clear bright eyes, a quiet shy face, the small bucket hanging easily from one hand, feet steady as if the chore is simple. Modest everyday cloth, full bodies, soft morning light.

結果：畫面幾乎沒有變矮。採用這張，並在 README 寫明身高偏差，不再重試。
